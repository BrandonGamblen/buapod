4 · Fins In design

One solid fin on every rib. Turning a ring at the top twists all fins together to shade the roof from the sun.

  • Fins: fixed at the root on the rib, from 100 mm above the seat; each widens toward the top.
  • Fin ring: Ø400 mm, 2.45 m above the seat, joined to the centre by 8 spokes. The fin tops attach to it.
  • Turning: from inside, by a hand wheel Ø400 mm 1.50 m above the seat, on a Ø80 mm tube up to the ring. Settings 0°, 15°, 30°, 45°.
  • Shade: at 45° the fins block about 95% of overhead sun.
  • At the door: the fin on the door rides on the top door leaf; the fin beside it springs back when the door opens.
