5 · Door In design

The door is one whole gore in wood, split into two hinged leaves.

  • Bottom leaf: shin, knee and seat. It hinges down at the floor and becomes a 2-step stair.
  • Steps: rises 265 mm; lower step 260 mm deep, upper step 116 mm deep, so the upper step never sticks out past the lower one.
  • Top leaf: the upper part of the door gore, on its own hinge.
  • Shoe area: the bottom leaf is where shoes are left. It is never covered by a cushion.
