7 · Electronics In design

Light from inside the ribs makes the glow band and roof glow at night.

  • Rib lights: LED tubes along the ribs, in the back corner, back wall and dome.
  • Under-glow: LED strips in the gill pockets under the pedestal, aimed down at the ground.
  • Colour: warm white, so the dark brown band reads amber.
