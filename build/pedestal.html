2 · Pedestal In design

One concrete pour from the top of the footing cone up to the base of the backrest: floor, shins, knee, seat and back corner, with 16 concrete fins (gills) underneath.

0.85 m³concrete
1.96 tweight
320 mmseat above the floor

Decided

  • Floor: flat, from the centre to the heel, 320 mm below the seat.
  • Knee: a smooth 90° turn, radius 100 mm, no bump. Seat flat for 700 mm.
  • Gills: a floor gill from the cone top out to the heel, and a knee gill under each knee, one per rib, curved.
  • Door section: left out of the concrete from floor level up. The door's own bottom leaf fills it.
  • Look: board or form-liner texture underneath; iron-oxide pigment to match the wood.
  • Top edge: a 60 mm ledge sloped 2–3° outward with a drip groove underneath. 16 cast-in inserts set from one template, the same template later used to stand the ribs.
  • Pour: 2 lifts on one day. First the floor and gills, then shin, knee, seat and back corner.
