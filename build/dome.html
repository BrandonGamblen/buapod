3 · Dome In design

16 wooden ribs standing on the pedestal, closed in three bands: a solid lower back, an amber glow band and a clear roof.

Ribs

  • Rubberwood sheet 20 mm, one layer, 140 mm deep, each rib cut in 3 pieces (5 sheets for the pod).
  • Each rib foot bolts to its pedestal insert through slotted holes (±10 mm), on a rubber pad, with a 10 mm gap to the concrete.
  • Crosspieces run rib to rib.

Walls and roof, bottom to top

BandMaterialFixing
Lower backHardwood sheet 6 mm inside and out, over crosspiecesGlued and pinned
Glow bandPolycarbonate 3 mm, dark brown (HomePro 1087215), 2 sheetsFloated in rib grooves on sealant
RoofPolycarbonate 3 mm, clear (HomePro 1087254), 2 sheets, 16 wedgesFloated in rib grooves; crosspieces 250 mm apart or closer; no screws through the sheet

Grooves: sheet thickness + 1 mm wide, 10 mm deep, leaving 1.4 mm room to expand per 0.5 m of panel.

Top

The top cap and fin ring are non-metal. A separate lightning terminal runs its own conductor down the outside to a ground rod.
